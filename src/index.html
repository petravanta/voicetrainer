How drills work

Pick a sound, start the microphone, then hold the sound steadily (or say the word a few times). The drill collects about a second of clean audio, scores it against typical values for that exact sound, and tells you which part of the sound to adjust.

Reading practice

Resonance -- Vowel -- Pitch -- Breath --

Scores place your measurements on a scale where 0 is the average adult male value and 100 the average adult female value for the same sound. Vowel references: Hillenbrand et al. (1995). Sibilant references are approximate centres of published ranges. Measurements are estimates; very high pitch (above about 280 Hz), background noise and phone microphones reduce accuracy. For best results use a headset or a quiet room, and turn off any voice effects on your microphone.

Send your speech for recognition?

Showing words uses your browser's speech recognition. In Chrome and Edge this sends the microphone audio to Google or Microsoft servers to turn it into text, while Show words is on and the microphone is running. Their privacy terms apply to that audio.

Everything else in VoiceTrainer stays on your device, and you can use the app fully without this. You can withdraw consent at any time.

Reading voice

Reading uses your browser's own speech voice, following the voice type, pitch and speed. Browsers do not let apps measure that voice, so it does not appear on the pitch chart or meters, and it never counts toward your progress.
