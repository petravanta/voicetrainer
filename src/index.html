How drills work

Pick a sound, start the microphone, then hold the sound steadily (or say the word a few times). The drill collects about a second of clean audio, scores it against typical values for that exact sound, and tells you which part of the sound to adjust.

Scores place your measurements on a scale where 0 is the average adult male value and 100 the average adult female value for the same sound. Vowel references: Hillenbrand et al. (1995). Sibilant references are approximate centres of published ranges. Measurements are estimates; very high pitch (above about 280 Hz), background noise and phone microphones reduce accuracy. For best results use a headset or a quiet room, and turn off any voice effects on your microphone.
